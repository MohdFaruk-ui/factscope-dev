My Automation Agent

A small helper I wrote for my own two YouTube channels.

Hi. I make short videos for two channels of my own: FactScope, which tells real stories about places and history, and Prompt Tadka, where I show people AI tools in Hindi and English.

Uploading every video by hand, filling in the title and description, setting the thumbnail and picking a publish time got slow, so I wrote a little program that runs on my own computer and does those steps for me. That program is "My Automation Agent". When it asks Google for permission, this is the name you see.

What it does

It uploads a finished video to my channel as a private video with a publish time, adds the title, description, tags and thumbnail I have written, and reads my channel's own numbers (views, how long people watched, which countries they came from) so I can see what is working.

Who it is for

Only me. It is not a service, there is nothing to sign up for, and it only works with the channels I own. It does not post anything I have not approved first.

Your data

How it handles Google account data is written plainly in the privacy policy. In short: it never sells, shares or sends my data anywhere else, and it never touches anyone else's account.
